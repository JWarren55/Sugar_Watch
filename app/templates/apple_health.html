1. Bring in your existing history

This includes the sleep, steps, active calories, weight, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, and workouts recorded in your Apple Health export.

  1. Connect your iPhone to Wi-Fi

    This file can be large. Keep your phone on Wi-Fi for the export and upload. Plug it in if your battery is low.

  2. Save the Apple Health export

    Open Health → your profile picture → Export All Health Data → Export. Choose Save to Files → On My iPhone, rather than iCloud Drive. Keep the original export.zip file; you do not need to unzip it. After the import succeeds, you can remove this ZIP from Files to free phone space; keep your records in Apple Health.

  3. Choose that file here

    Keep this page open until it says the import finished. The upload limit is 100 MB. Sugar Watch saves the supported records and removes its temporary ZIP copy.

What gets saved?

Individual samples are kept, including their time and recording source. Sleep stages are kept separately. Workouts keep the details available in the export, such as duration, energy, and distance. Re-importing the file does not create repeat copies of the same native-export records.

Other Apple Health categories, medical documents, GPS routes, and photos are not imported. Glucose still comes from Libre. Deleting something in Apple Health does not automatically delete it here.

2. Set up small daily uploads

Apple Shortcuts can send sleep, steps, active calories, weight, and heart measurements. Workouts need a new Apple Health export when you want to update them.

One-time iPhone setup is required. We do not have a signed shortcut to install with one tap. The guide walks you through building it in Apple's Shortcuts app.

Run it once a day while on Wi-Fi. It checks for recent syncs before reading Health, sends at most 200 samples per request, and saves progress only after every batch arrives. Missed uploads stay in Apple Health and can be sent later.

The shortcut must check Wi-Fi before any network request. That check reduces cellular use; a Wi-Fi drop can still change the phone's route. For strict zero-cellular use, run with Airplane Mode on and Wi-Fi turned back on. The website cannot force iOS to use Wi-Fi.

Automatic runs and battery use

After a manual test works, in Shortcuts choose Automation → + → Charger → Is Connected, select Run Immediately if offered, and run your Sugar Watch shortcut. Its first actions must stop unless Wi-Fi is connected.

The shortcut skips categories synced in the last 20 hours. Apple can block Health access while the phone is locked; unlock your phone and tap the shortcut if a run is missed. There is no continuous polling or background retry loop.
